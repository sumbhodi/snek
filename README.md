# snek

Snake. One HTML file. No build step. No dependencies. Fits in an email.

Reach 108 and the snake stops dying.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser. That's the whole install.

## Controls

- Arrow keys or WASD — move
- Space — start, and pause
- Touch — the d-pad; the center button pauses
- Pause menu — Continue, Save, Restart. Save reloads your snake next visit.

## What happens at 108

Normal mode is classic snake. Three levels. You die.

Score 108 → nirvana. You pick a path:

- **Immortal** — walls wrap, nothing kills you. Speed and size adjust from the pause menu.
- **Bodhisattva** — back to mortal, keep your score.
- **Reincarnation** — small snake again, keep your score.

The background pulses at theta frequency. Eating is 440hz, nirvana is 432hz. This is on purpose. It's a flow-state delivery mechanism disguised as a 1990s arcade game. You cannot lose. You can only keep going.
